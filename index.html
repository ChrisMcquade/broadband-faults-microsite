Start with the basics

These safe checks are included to make the microsite feel like a real broadband-faults entry point. The assistant can take over if the issue continues.

◉

Check your Hub lights

Look at the power and connection lights and note their colour or whether they are flashing.

↻

Restart your Hub

Switch it off, leave it briefly, then switch it on and allow it to reconnect fully.

⌁

Check cables

Make sure the power and broadband cables are secure and have not been damaged or pulled loose.
